40mm Button by paedia on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6555188

Summary:
Sturdy 40mm Button. The clever scissor mechanism evenly distributes the pressure across the entire contact surface. The built-in microswitch is reliably activated even when pressed in the corner. The button is very robust and can also be used as a foot switch.Assembly: First, snap the scissor parts onto the base, then insert the microswitch and snap on the cover.Additionally, you’ll need:1 standard 5A microswitch (Omron SS-5 F or similar)2 brass inserts (M3, 5mm)2 countersunk screws (M3, 6mm)
