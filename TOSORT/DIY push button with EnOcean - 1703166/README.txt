DIY push button with EnOcean by Svenergy on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:1703166

Summary:
Today I provide the most simple housing mechanic for realizing a customized push button for EnOcean´s PTM switch module. The button model should be modified to individual needs or shape to realize an unique push button. The base part is already finished and fits to the PTM module and to inner shape of the button part.I have modeled this basically to generate funny motive switches like shown in the attached pictures. Some of the motive buttons may be added to thingiverse later by me. I also would like to see all your funny ideas of how a push button switch should look like :-)Be aware that this simple model just actuates the PTM Module without any channel information. The module will send just pushed or released. Most of the receivers need a channel information to work correctly. To realize channel information in the radio telegram add an adequate pin in your design and play with its correct length of that pin.
