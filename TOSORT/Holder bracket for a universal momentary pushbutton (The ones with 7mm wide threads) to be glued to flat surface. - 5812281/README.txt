Holder bracket for a universal momentary pushbutton (The ones with 7mm wide threads) to be glued to flat surface. by Rinoa on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5812281

Summary:
YouTube video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gnQClMuNEI4I got some of those tiny universal pushbuttons but didn't have any enclosures, or brackets to hold them for a project. I made this real quick so at least I wouldn't be fumbling pressing the buttons while I rapid-prototype my circuit. Maybe you should print this for yourself the next time you have a pushbutton dangling from your test arduino!
