Panel switch by mrice on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:1104383

Summary:
This is a simple panel switch you can print, which uses a standard mechanical endstop (snap-action switch).  To assemble, first put the button piece inside the housing, then use two M2x16mm or similar screws &amp; nuts to mount the limit switch inside the larger housing so that the lever arm of the limit switch is against the back of the push button.  Easy!
