Summary:
Tactile Switch KeycapA flat 13mm square keycap to suit a round 6mm tactile button. Specifically designed for the miniPET tactile button keyboard.  Included is both the square button for the single size keycaps and the larger double size keycap for Space and Return. Design ToolI have included the source FreeCAD files for modification if wanted and the stl files. Printing HintsI used PLA at 100% fill and the highest resolution my Ender 3 Pro can do to print themKeycap labels.The Word document is a template for printing out on to a sticker to label the keycaps for the miniPET keyboard. Hints and Warnings. After testing on the miniPET i would suggest these are NOT good for computer keyboard use. Small low use tactile switches used in frequently, yes, but a heavily used computer keyboard NO. You are welcome to use the files here to try and improve them, but after using these on my miniPET it is clear they are not very good for that usage.if you are to use these with the miniPET keyboard or any other design, make sure the tactile butons are soldered perfectly square and flat on the board. The keycaps amplify any imperfect layout. for the miniPET i used ordinary packaging label (1 per sheet) on my injet. I used scissors to cut each out to stick on the caps. It is slow and tedious as the backing is a pain to peel off.
