Small Accessible Button by DominicFromReplayforKids on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6140307

Summary:
This is a cheap and easy accessible button intended to be used to operate toys but capable of performing a broad range of functions.The button is made of three parts that fit together simply as well as two external parts(the switch and wire) that can be purchased separately and need to be soldered together then hot glued in place.For more information visit us at www.replayforkids.org
