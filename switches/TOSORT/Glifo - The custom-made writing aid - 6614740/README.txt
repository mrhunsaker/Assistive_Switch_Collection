Glifo - The custom-made writing aid by UNI_CO on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6614740

Summary:
The custom-made writing aid Glifo was created by Opendot and TOG to help children with a complex neurological disorder write and draw so that they could leave their marks.This accessibility tool can be useful also for elder people, adults with arthritis or in general for people and kids with difficulties in grasping small objects.Compatibility:Pens, marker or any cylindrical object ranging from 6 mm to 14 mm in diameterFinger ranging from 10 mm in diameter up to 24 mm
